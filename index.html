About

KAZMANIA LLC is a software company based in Maryland, USA. We create mobile and web applications, from the first sketch to the version in your hands, and we keep improving them long after launch.

Our approach is straightforward: build software that does its job well, keep the interface clear, and treat the information people trust us with carefully. Wherever we can, we design our apps so your data stays on your device, and we're upfront about anything that doesn't.

Common questions

How do I get help with an app?

Email support@kazmania.dev and include:

  • The name of the app and its version
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Screenshots, if they help
How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a request.

How do I delete my data?

Our apps are designed to keep your data on your device, so you can remove it inside the app or by uninstalling it. To ask about anything we hold, email us. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Where do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us at the address above. We read every message.

How do I reach you about a business inquiry?

Use the same email address and tell us a little about what you have in mind.
