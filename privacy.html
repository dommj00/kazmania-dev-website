Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Our core privacy promise

We build our apps with privacy at the foundation. Wherever possible, your personal content stays on your device or in your own cloud storage, and we do not collect or store it on our servers.

Introduction

KAZMANIA LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is a software company based in Maryland, USA. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit kazmania.dev, contact us, or use our mobile and web applications. If an app has its own privacy notice, that notice applies to the app and adds to this policy.

Information we do not collect or access

Unless an app's own notice says otherwise, we do not collect or access:

  • Your personal content: the information you create in our apps, such as entries, ratings, notes, and any insights the app generates
  • Your usage patterns: how you use an app, which features you open, or your behavior within it
  • Location data
  • Device information: such as device identifiers or hardware details
  • Photos, media, or contacts

Information we may collect

When you contact us. If you email support@kazmania.dev, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve our products.

Website server logs. Our web host may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. This website has no accounts, advertising, or tracking scripts. It does load a font from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when you visit.

App Store information. We may receive basic information from Apple or our subscription provider about downloads and subscription status, such as whether a subscription is active, the app version, and your country or region. We do not receive your payment details.

How your data is stored

On your device

The personal data you create in our apps is stored on your device using secure local storage.

In your personal cloud

If you turn on device backup or sync features, such as iCloud, an app's data may be saved to your personal cloud account. That storage is:

  • Controlled entirely by you
  • Protected according to your cloud provider's security standards
  • Not accessible to us
  • Governed by your cloud provider's privacy policy

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share limited information only with service providers that help us operate, such as our web host, email provider, and the services that process purchases and subscriptions, or when the law requires it.

Your rights and control

You own your data. You can delete the content you create by removing it inside an app or by uninstalling the app. To ask us to delete information we hold about you, such as a support email, contact us at the address below. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and for reasonable record-keeping.

Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

California privacy rights (CCPA)

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We collect minimal personal information, we do not sell it, and you keep direct control over the information stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the dates at the top of this page.
